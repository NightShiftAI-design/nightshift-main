Overview

What cookies are & how we use them

The basics on what cookies do and the specific ways NightShift AI uses them on this site.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, keep sessions stable, and understand usage patterns — making your experience consistent across visits.

✅

How we use cookies

We use cookies for a narrow set of purposes only:

  • Basic website functionality and performance
  • Security and reliability (e.g. rate limiting, abuse prevention)
  • Understanding which pages are visited most, in aggregate
  • Improving content and overall user experience

What we do not do

We are committed to keeping your data yours:

  • No behavioral or targeted advertising
  • No cross-site tracking or profiling
  • No selling or renting of visitor data
  • No fingerprinting or persistent identifiers for ads
Details

Cookie types, third parties & your controls

Expand any section for a full breakdown. We've kept this as straightforward as possible.

Cookie types we may use Essential + analytics

Essential cookies — Required for core site functions including navigation, form submissions, and security features. These cannot be disabled without affecting site functionality.

Preference cookies — Remember optional choices you make, such as your theme preference (dark or light mode). These are only set when you actively make a selection.

Analytics cookies — Help us understand usage patterns in aggregate so we can improve pages and content. These are not used for advertising and are not intended to identify you personally.

Third-party services Limited use

We may use hosting, security, or basic analytics providers that set limited cookies necessary for reliability and performance monitoring. These cookies are not used for advertising or cross-site profiling.

If we ever add additional analytics tools that change how cookies are used, we will update this policy and — where required — provide a clear choice mechanism before any new cookies are set.

Managing cookies in your browser Your control

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View which cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies at once
  • Block third-party cookies from being set
  • Block all cookies (though this may affect site functionality)

Disabling cookies may affect some features — for example, your theme preference (dark or light mode) won't be remembered between visits. Core site functionality will remain available.

Changes to this policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Service, applicable law, or how our providers operate. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

For major changes that affect how cookies are used, we'll provide a clear notice on the site before the changes take effect.
