Questions
Frequently asked
What is the setup fee for?

The setup fee covers custom AI script development, voice configuration, policy training, agent provisioning, and your onboarding session. Work begins immediately after payment and the fee is non-refundable once your agent is configured.

How does overage billing work exactly?

Every call is logged with its exact duration. On the 1st of each month, we calculate your previous month's total minutes. If you exceeded your plan's limit, the overage is automatically charged to your card on file at your plan's per-minute rate. You can see current usage in your dashboard at any time before the month ends.

How many minutes will I actually use?

A typical 20–40 room motel using NightShift for after-hours coverage uses 400–700 minutes per month — well within the Core 1,000 min limit. A 40–80 room property running 24/7 typically uses 1,000–1,800 minutes. Pro's 2,000 min handles most high-volume properties without overage.

Is the first month charged with the setup fee?

Yes. Your first charge covers the setup fee plus your first month's subscription together. Core: $299 + $549 = $848 on day one. Pro: $449 + $699 = $1,148 on day one. From month two onward, only the monthly subscription is charged automatically to your saved card.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel with 7 days written notice before your next billing date and no further charges will apply. Setup fees and monthly fees are non-refundable. No long-term contracts required.
