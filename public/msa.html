1. Master Subscription Agreement v2

This Agreement is between NightShift AI LLC ("Provider") and the subscribing hotel operator ("Customer"). It governs Customer's use of Provider's AI-powered hotel front desk service under Delaware law.

1.1 Services

Provider delivers an AI voice agent that answers guest calls (under 3 seconds), assists with reservations via secure handoff, and escalates issues to hotel staff. No credit card numbers are collected. Implementation is fast — typically under 10 minutes.

1.3 Payment Terms

Invoices are due net 30 days. Late payments incur interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law. Provider may suspend service for non-payment after notice.

1.4 Term & Termination

Initial term per Order Form; auto-renews annually unless either party gives 60 days written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days notice to cure. All accrued fees remain payable.

1.5 Intellectual Property

Provider retains all rights to the software, AI models, and improvements. Customer acquires only a limited license to use the service. Customer data remains Customer's property.

1.6 Warranties & Disclaimers

Service is provided "AS IS." Provider makes no guarantee of AI accuracy, revenue, bookings, or guest satisfaction. AI may produce incorrect responses. Customer remains responsible for all guest communications and outcomes.

1.7 Limitation of Liability

Provider's total liability shall not exceed fees paid in the preceding 12 months. No liability for lost profits, lost revenue, guest dissatisfaction, or business interruption.

1.8 Indemnification

Customer shall indemnify Provider for claims arising from: guest communications, ADA violations, TCPA violations, reservation disputes, data consent failures, and franchise/brand claims. Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party IP infringement claims caused by the service technology.

1.9 Customer Obligations

  • Accuracy of all hotel information provided
  • Obtaining legally required call recording consent
  • ADA compliance and accessible communication accommodations
  • Reservation policies and all guest communications
  • Compliance with franchise brand standards (if applicable)

1.10 Governing Law

Delaware law governs. Exclusive venue in Delaware state or federal courts.

4. ADA & Service Animal Insert

Effective Communication

Customer is responsible for providing auxiliary aids (TTY, relay services, interpreters) as required by ADA Title III. AI voice may transfer to staff or provide text-relay routing if configured.

Service Animal Rules (DOJ Guidance)

Provider's AI scripts are configured to follow DOJ guidance. Only two questions are permitted:

  1. Is the animal required because of a disability?
  2. What work or task has it been trained to perform?

No other inquiries are permitted. Customer is responsible for ensuring hotel staff compliance.

Escalation Requirement

AI must escalate all ADA accommodation requests, service animal disputes, and accessibility complaints to hotel staff immediately.

Liability

Provider is not liable for ADA violations unless caused solely by platform malfunction. Customer indemnifies Provider for all ADA guest claims.
