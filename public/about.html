A virtual front desk built for reliability — not AI hype.

NightShift AI is trained on your property's policies, procedures, and escalation rules. Designed to answer after-hours calls professionally, resolve routine issues, and route urgent situations to the right person — consistently.

Why

After-hours is where hotels
lose money — and create risk

Missed calls turn into missed bookings. Overnight shifts are hard to staff. Even when someone is onsite, calls pile up during busy moments.

📵

Missed bookings

Guests often call once. If no one answers, they book somewhere else — and you never know it happened.

🔀

Inconsistent answers

Policies and rates get explained differently depending on who picks up — at 2am vs 2pm.

🌙

Overnight staffing strain

Hiring, training, and retention for night coverage is expensive and unpredictable.

⚠️

Unclear escalation

Urgent situations need safe handling and fast routing — not guesswork or a missed call.

📞

Operational overload

Busy check-in windows and peak moments create call pileups — even during the day.

⭐

Reputation risk

Bad guest experiences compound fast when issues aren't handled consistently and quickly.

Safety + control built in

NightShift is configured with structured workflows for sensitive situations, consistent escalation rules, and privacy-minded handling — routing payments through your existing process, never collecting card numbers. For ADA-sensitive scenarios, you can require escalation to staff so a human makes the final call.
