Summary

Key points

Here are the main takeaways. Full details follow in the sections below — they're the sections that control.

NightShift AI is a virtual front desk trained on your property's rules — not a generic call center. Hotels control configuration choices: what the AI can answer, what gets logged, and who receives escalations.

  • ✓
    We do not sell your data — We use it to operate the Service and support your hotel or business.
  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

Do not send sensitive data

Please do not send full card numbers, passwords, or government IDs via the contact form. NightShift is designed to avoid collecting card numbers via AI calls.

Data Collection

What we collect

We collect information necessary to operate NightShift and deliver services to hotels, motelss, and other supported businesses.

Account & Business Information

When you sign up or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, and role or title.

Configuration Data

To train your virtual front desk, we collect your property address, amenities, policies (pets, deposits, smoking, weekly rates), and escalation contacts and routing rules.

Call Metadata

For every handled call, we may log the caller number, time, duration, and high-level outcome tags to support operational reviews and follow-up.

Call Content

Depending on your configuration, we may generate transcripts, summaries, or structured notes for logging and staff follow-up. These help your team know what happened overnight without listening to every call.

Reservation Details

When guests inquire about availability, we capture guest name, dates, nights, room type, and guest count. Workflows are configured to avoid collecting payment card numbers.

Website Usage Data

We collect basic data about pages visited, device type, and browser to understand how the site is used and improve content. This is aggregate and not used for behavioral advertising.

Use & Sharing

How we use it & who we share it with

We use data only to operate NightShift and support your property. We do not sell your data.

How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Service
  • Answer, route, and summarize calls for your property
  • Send email notifications based on your configuration
  • Provide customer support and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve reliability and performance of configured workflows
  • Monitor for abuse, fraud, and security risks

We process information based on legitimate business needs to provide the Service, comply with law, and protect security.

Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information with trusted providers to operate NightShift, such as:

  • Infrastructure & hosting — secure cloud hosting and storage providers
  • Telephony — call routing and phone infrastructure
  • Messaging — email notification providers
  • Analytics — understanding website performance in aggregate
  • Integrations — PMS, CRM, or tools you choose to connect
  • Legal — when required to comply with law or protect rights or safety

Payment Card Data (PCI)

NightShift is designed to avoid collecting or storing guest payment card numbers through AI voice agents. If a workflow requires payment, we route to your existing secure process — front desk, payment link, or your preferred method.

Call Recording & Consent

Call handling laws vary by jurisdiction. Calls may be recorded or transcribed based on your configuration. Hotel customers are responsible for providing required caller disclosures and obtaining necessary consent under applicable laws.

Service Provider Role

For hotels using NightShift, we typically process caller and guest information on your behalf. You control configuration, routing, and logging. We do not use caller or guest data for independent marketing.

Security & Rights

How we protect data & your rights

Security measures in place, and what rights you have over the data we hold about you.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect information, including access controls, restricted internal access, and secure cloud infrastructure. No system is 100% secure, but we continuously improve our security posture.

For full details, see our Data Security page.

ADA Alignment & Safety

NightShift can be configured with ADA-aware service animal handling patterns and safety escalation rules. Hotels control the exact policies, fees, and escalation contacts used for each call flow.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your data, and to restrict or object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact support@nightshifthotels.com.

Data Retention

We retain call logs, summaries, transcripts (if enabled), and configuration data as needed to provide the Service, support your business, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary by configuration.

Upon request, we may delete or anonymize certain data, subject to legal and operational requirements.

International Transfers

If you access NightShift from outside the United States, your information may be processed and stored in the U.S. or other locations where our service providers operate, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Children's Privacy

NightShift AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can remove it.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and to understand site usage. We do not use cookies for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Service or applicable laws. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the Service means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your rights? Reach out at support@nightshifthotels.com or via our contact page.
